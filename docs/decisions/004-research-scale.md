# 004 — Research scale


## Why

The 6-agent arena is a live show. "Go deep on a bigger idea" does not need that crowd.

Most unique value sat in the researcher and the frame-challenger. Extra pairs often reached the same verdict by a different route.

## What we did

Add `scale=research` to the debate skill. `mode` is still epistemic vs dnd.

Research uses one tension pair, a frame-challenger, and the strawman. Event cap 20. Soft stop after one POSITION and about four follow-ups. All seats `sonnet`. No blind-spot finder.

Arena is unchanged and still the default.

## What we did not do

We did not delete arena.

We did not add OpenCode.

We did not change the live UI.

## Upstream

New scale, same store. Safe to offer after the clock PR.
