# 002 — Short briefing


## Why

The old judge write-up was a dissertation. Rankings, process scores, and event stats buried the answer.

People wanted: what held, what to challenge, what would change my mind, what to do next.

## What we did

All three skills now present the same short briefing in chat:

1. What Survived
2. What To Challenge
3. Killshot
4. Framing
5. Residual Disagreement
6. Next Steps

The debate judge still scores the hard rules. Those scores go in an appendix, and in the Results overlay. Chat does not paste them.

The briefing above the appendix must stay under 600 words.

Appendix rankings keep the old `### 1st — {name}` headers. The Results overlay still parses those. A table would not fill rank badges.

## What we did not do

We did not change how agents argue.

We did not change the live UI layout.

We did not add OpenCode support.

## Upstream

Helps Claude users today. No host change. Safe to offer as its own PR.
